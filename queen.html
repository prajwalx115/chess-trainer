The Queen
9 Points — Most Powerful Piece
About the Queen

The Queen is the most powerful piece on the board. She combines the full movement of the Rook and Bishop, allowing her to sweep across the board in any direction for any number of squares.

Losing the Queen is almost always catastrophic. Players guard her carefully while using her sweeping power to create threats, control open lines, and deliver checkmate.

Movement Rules
  • Moves any number of squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • Combines the movement of Rook + Bishop in a single piece.
  • Cannot jump over other pieces — her path must be clear.
  • Controls up to 27 squares from the center of the board.
  • Can capture any enemy piece along her movement path.
