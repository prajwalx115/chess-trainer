The King
∞ Value — Most Important Piece
About the King

The King is the heart of the game. Though not the strongest piece in terms of mobility, its survival is everything. The moment your King is checkmated, the game is over. Every decision in chess ultimately serves one purpose — protecting your King while threatening the opponent's.

In the endgame, the King transforms from a piece to be sheltered into a powerful active fighter, marching to the center to support pawns and dominate the board.

Movement Rules
  • Moves exactly one square in any direction — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • Cannot move into a square attacked by an enemy piece — this would be an illegal move.
  • Can capture an adjacent enemy piece only if that square is not defended.
  • May perform Castling — a special move with the Rook if neither piece has moved and no pieces are between them.
  • When in check, must move out of check — cannot stay in danger.
