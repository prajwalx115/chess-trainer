The Knight
3 Points — Minor Piece
About the Knight

The Knight is a unique jumping piece. It is the only piece that can jump over other pieces, making it highly versatile in crowded positions. A knight moves in an L-shape — two squares in one direction and then one square perpendicular to that.

Knights are particularly strong in the center of the board, where they can control up to eight squares. They are often used to create tactical threats and can be very effective in both opening and endgame scenarios.

Movement Rules
  • Moves in an L-shape: two squares in one direction, then one square perpendicular.
  • Can jump over other pieces.
  • Captures on the square it lands on.
  • Controls up to 8 squares from the center.
  • Very strong in closed positions.
