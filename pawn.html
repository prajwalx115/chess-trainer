The Pawn
1 Point — The Soul of Chess
About the Pawn

According to the great player Philidor, "Pawns are the soul of chess." Each player starts with eight pawns — more than any other piece. Individually weak, their collective pawn structure defines the entire character of the position.

A pawn that reaches the opposite end of the board promotes — it becomes any piece the player chooses (almost always a Queen). This makes passed pawns — pawns with no opposing pawns blocking them — incredibly dangerous in the endgame.

Movement Rules
  • Moves forward only — one square at a time in normal play.
  • On its very first move, a pawn may advance two squares forward.
  • Captures diagonally forward — one square to the left or right.
  • En passant: if an enemy pawn moves two squares and lands beside yours, you may capture it as if it moved one square.
  • Promotion: reaching the 8th rank transforms the pawn into any piece (Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight).
