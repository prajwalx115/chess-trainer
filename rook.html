The Rook
5 Points — Major Piece
About the Rook

The Rook is a major piece — one of the two most powerful piece types alongside the Queen. It excels on open files (columns with no pawns blocking) and can control entire ranks and files from afar.

Rooks are typically activated later in the game once pawns clear the way. In the endgame they become dominant, often working together to deliver checkmate with the famous Ladder Mate.

Movement Rules
  • Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically.
  • Cannot move diagonally — straight lines only.
  • Cannot jump over other pieces — path must be clear.
  • Participates in Castling with the King (if neither has moved).
  • Controls up to 14 squares from anywhere on the board.
