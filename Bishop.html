The Bishop
3 Points — Minor Piece
About the Bishop

The Bishop is a long-range diagonal piece. Each player begins with two bishops — one on light squares and one on dark squares. A bishop can never change the color of its square, making it "color-bound."

Two bishops working together — the "bishop pair" — are extremely powerful in open positions, covering both colors of the board. A bishop is strongest when pawns are on the opposite color, leaving its diagonals wide open.

Movement Rules
  • Moves any number of squares diagonally in any of four diagonal directions.
  • Always stays on the same color square it started on — forever.
  • Cannot jump over other pieces — path must be clear.
  • Two bishops on opposite colors together cover the whole board.
  • Controls up to 13 squares from the center of the board.
